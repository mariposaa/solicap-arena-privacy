Solicap Arena - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

This policy explains what data the app collects, why it is collected, and the choices you have. It applies to the Solicap Arena mobile application (shown in stores and in the app as SoliLife).

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information (email address, display name, user ID)
  • Gameplay statistics and in-app progress
  • Content you choose to create or upload (posts, photos, comments)
  • Approximate and precise location, only when you use a location-based feature (see section 2)
  • Technical and diagnostic data (device model, app version, crash and performance logs)

2. Location Data

Some features of the app work with your device location. This section describes exactly how that data is handled.

Location is collected only while you are using the app, and only after you grant permission. The app does not collect, track, or receive your location in the background or when the app is closed.

What is collected. Approximate location and precise location, obtained from your device once at the moment you use one of the features listed below. The app does not run continuous location tracking, does not record location history, and does not use geofencing or background location services.

Why it is collected. Location is used solely to make location-dependent features work:

  • Showing cultural places, restaurants and points of interest near you
  • Suggesting nearby users and local content in the discovery features, when you choose to use them
  • Sharing your location with contacts you select yourself in the personal safety feature

When it is collected. Only when you open one of those features and confirm the request. If you decline the permission, the rest of the app continues to work; only the location-dependent parts are unavailable.

Who can see it. Your precise coordinates are not displayed to other users. Discovery features show only approximate distance or your city or district. In the safety feature, your location is shared only with the specific people you choose, and only for as long as you keep that sharing active.

How long it is kept. Location taken for nearby search is used to answer that request and is not stored as a location history on our servers. Where a feature you started requires it (for example a discovery post you publish or an active safety share), the associated location is kept only while that content or session exists and is deleted when you delete it.

Your control. You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings (Settings → Apps → SoliLife → Permissions → Location on Android; Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services on iOS). Revoking permission stops all location collection immediately.

Not sold, not used for advertising. Location data is never sold, and it is not used to build advertising profiles or shared with advertising networks.

3. How We Use Data

  • Provide core gameplay features
  • Operate location-based features as described in section 2
  • Improve app performance, security, and user experience

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses Firebase services provided by Google, including Authentication and Analytics. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies.

Location data is processed within the app and our own backend. It is not sent to advertising partners.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above, with other users where you have chosen to publish or share content, and where required by law.

6. Data Retention

Account data is kept while your account is active. Content you publish is kept until you delete it or it expires under the feature's own rules. When you delete your account, associated personal data is removed.
